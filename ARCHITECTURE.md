# VolleyCore Product

## Purpose

VolleyCore is a volleyball club management platform for athletes, families, categories, training schedules, events, calendars, fees and payments.

## Current organization

- Organization: ASBAVOL
- Organization ID: `asbavol`

## Primary users

- Administrator
- Treasurer
- Parent / guardian
- Athlete

## Product principles

1. A user is not the same as a family.
2. A family may contain multiple users and multiple athletes.
3. An athlete may participate in multiple categories.
4. Financial records belong to the athlete and may be viewed by authorized family members.
5. Training schedules and events are separate concepts.
6. Production must remain stable while changes are tested in `develop`.
